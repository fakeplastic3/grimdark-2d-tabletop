## Material still required

Obtain lawful access to the complete Core Rules, the currently listed Universal Rules Updates, October Space Marine amendments, current Codex: Space Marines and Ultramarines material, current Codex: Orks and amendments, October MFM values, and the Chapter Approved 2026–27 mission cards/layouts. Verify each implemented rule against the applicable combination, retaining page/section and revision references. Public previews verify particular differences but cannot certify a whole match.

No downloaded rulebook, copyrighted card artwork, miniature image, proprietary PDF, or third-party roster database is included in this repository. A free download is not automatically a redistribution licence. The original-code licence does not grant rights to Games Workshop content. Private imports remain private and do not acquire an official verification label merely by importing successfully.

## Search hygiene

Search also returned 2024 and earlier 10th Edition Core Rules and unrelated Age of Sigmar material. Those documents were excluded. Third-party cheat sheets and migration projects were not accepted as authoritative rules sources. Their existence does not resolve missing primary evidence.
